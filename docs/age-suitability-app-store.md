# Age suitability — Chit Chat Social

**Purpose:** Publish this page on your public site (for example `https://chitchat.app/age-suitability`), then paste that URL into App Store Connect → Age Rating → Age Suitability URL (optional).

## Who this app is for

Chit Chat Social is a **social platform** with **user-generated content**, **messaging**, and **advertising**. It is **not intended for young children**.

The App Store age rating for this app reflects **typical content and features**. **Parents and guardians** should review the rating and decide whether the app is appropriate for anyone in their care.

## Recommended minimum age

We recommend use by individuals **16 and older**, consistent with the app’s **App Store age rating** and the nature of open **community content** and **direct messaging**.

## What users may encounter

- Content created by **other users** (text, photos, video, audio).
- **Direct messages** and social interaction with other people.
- **Third-party advertising** and promotional content.

We provide **safety tools**, **reporting**, and **policies** to reduce harmful content, but we **cannot guarantee** that all content will always be appropriate for every viewer.

## Parental guidance

We do **not** provide full **parental control** software inside the app. Parents and guardians should use **device-level** parental controls (Screen Time on Apple devices, Family Sharing, etc.) and talk with teens about **safe online behavior**.

## Contact

For safety or policy questions, use the contact method listed in the app or on your official support page.
